HackRadar privacy policy

Effective 12 October 2026 · HackRadar is a free Android app that lists hackathons for students in India.

Delete your account and data: in the app open Profile → Delete my account. Everything tied to you (account, preferences, saved hackathons, alert settings, push address) is removed at once and reminders on your phone are cancelled. You can also email atulshxrma04@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and it is done within 7 days.

What we collect

  • A random account ID, made automatically the first time you open the app, so your settings and saved hackathons can be stored. No sign-in is needed for this.
  • If you sign in (Google, GitHub or a code by email): your email address, and from Google or GitHub your name and profile picture link.
  • Your preferences: how you want to join (online/offline), fee preference, degree, the city you choose, and how far you can travel.
  • Location, only if you use it: when you tap "Use my location", the position is rounded to about 1 km before it is saved or sent anywhere. Your exact GPS position is never stored.
  • Saved hackathons and whether you marked them Interested or Registered.
  • A push notification address for this phone, if you turn on new-hackathon alerts.
  • Reports and submissions you send: a "Report wrong info" reason and note, or the details of a hackathon you submit. An approved submission is shown publicly as a listing; your name is not.

We do not collect contacts, photos, files, phone numbers or payment details. There are no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs, and we never sell or share your data for advertising.

Why

  • To show hackathons ranked for you, with distance from your city.
  • To keep your saved hackathons and settings on any phone you sign in on.
  • To send the alerts and reminders you turn on.
  • To fix wrong listings and review submitted hackathons.

Who processes it

  • Supabase: database and sign-in (stores the data above).
  • Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: deliver push notifications and app updates.
  • Google or GitHub: only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Gmail: sends the sign-in code if you sign in with email.
  • Photon (komoot, OpenStreetMap data): receives the text you type in city search, or your rounded position, to suggest places. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

These services may store data on servers outside India. Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and our database only lets each person read and change their own rows.

Hackathon listings

Hackathon details come from the public pages of Unstop, Devfolio, Hack2Skill, HackerEarth, ETHGlobal and Hackster, and from reviewed submissions. Every listing links to its official page; registration happens there, not in HackRadar.

How long we keep it

As long as you use the app. Deleting your account removes it immediately. A guest account that is never used again can be deleted after 12 months.

Your choices

  • Use the app without signing in.
  • Turn alerts and reminders off in Profile, or in your phone's settings.
  • Change or clear your city and preferences in Profile at any time.
  • Delete your account as described at the top.

Children

HackRadar is meant for college students and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date. Questions: atulshxrma04@gmail.com.
